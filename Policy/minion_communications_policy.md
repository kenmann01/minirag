# Communications Policy (for Minions)

**Organization:** Gru's Evil Lair & Associates
**Document ID:** LAIR-POL-019
**Effective Date:** February 10, 2024
**Last Reviewed:** February 10, 2024
**Owner:** Dr. Nefario (he monitors the channels; he says it is for safety; it is also for entertainment)

## 1. Purpose

This policy covers how the lair talks, radios, and occasionally announces things. Clear communication prevents misunderstandings. Most of the lair's craters began as a misunderstanding, and one began as a "read receipt."

## 2. Official Channels

Lair business runs on three channels: Mission (urgent), Operations (daily), and Banter (everything else, within reason). Channel discipline matters; the last operational update delivered over Banter was received as a joke, executed as a joke, and billed to the lair as a joke. The channel guide is posted beside every radio, laminated, because the first guide was not laminated and is now a hat.

## 3. Mission Communications

During an active operation, the Mission channel is kept clear. Status reports use the short format: location, status, bananas (a count, not a mood). Anyone may call "hold hold hold" to pause the plan. Stuart has used this to pause a heist for a snack and now requires Squad Leader countersignature, effective immediately, retroactively, and forever.

## 4. Response Expectations

Messages on Operations are answered within one business day, and remote arrangements follow the Remote Work Policy (LAIR-POL-014). During active operations, Mission-channel calls are acknowledged within fifteen minutes. "I was in the vents" is an explanation, not an acknowledgment, and the vents have their own channel now.

## 5. Codenames

Open channels use codenames. Real names, lair coordinates, and the word "shrink" together in one sentence are prohibited. The codename registry is maintained by Dr. Nefario; requests are processed in the order received and Minion may not be "Cool Kevin." It is taken. It has always been taken.

## 6. External and Personal Communications

Minion does not post about work. No blogs, no podcasts, no "vague-posting that could only be about a lair." Family and friends may be told Minion works "in logistics," which is true in the way that matters: things move, money is involved, nobody asks twice. Photographs taken inside the facility are covered by the Visitors Policy (LAIR-POL-020) and by the look Dr. Nefario gives when a flash goes off.

## 7. Device Hygiene

Channel devices are lair equipment under the Equipment Policy (LAIR-POL-016): cleaned, charged, and never used to order forty pizzas to a rival's hideout "as a distraction." It worked once. It will never work again, and the pizzeria has our description.

## 8. Emergency Broadcasts

Emergency broadcasts override every channel, repeat twice, and end with the all-clear phrase, which is changed monthly and is never, under any circumstances, "banana." The reasons are in a sealed file labeled simply: Stuart, 2022. When the broadcast repeats, it means repeat, not reply, and the channel stays quiet until the all-clear.

## 9. Questions

Direct questions to Dr. Nefario on Operations, or in person, ideally not while he is holding the vat slide.
