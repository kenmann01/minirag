# Expense Reimbursement Policy (for Minions)

**Organization:** Gru's Evil Lair & Associates
**Document ID:** LAIR-POL-009
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024
**Last Reviewed:** January 15, 2024
**Owner:** Dr. Nefario (Gru signs it, Nefario actually reads it)

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains what lair will pay Minion back for, and what lair will absolutely not, no matter how good the reason sounded at 2 AM when Minion thought of it.

## 2. General Principles

All expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and have receipt. Expenses under $25 (or 25 bananas, exchange rate fluctuates) need only written description. Description "for evil purposes" is not specific enough. We need to know WHICH evil purpose.

## 3. Submission and Deadlines

Expense reports submitted within **60 days**. Late submission requires written approval from Dr. Nefario, who will look at you over his glasses for a long, long time before signing.

## 4. Approval Thresholds

- **Under $500:** Minion Squad Leader approval only.
- **$500–$2,000:** Squad Leader + Dr. Nefario approval.
- **Over $2,000:** Gru approval required, in person, said with full dramatic villain monologue energy whether you want it or not.
- **Anything involving unauthorized weapon prototypes:** Triggers **Villain Protocol** immediately, no exceptions, this is how we lost the second sub-basement.

## 5. Travel Expenses

- **Mileage (Minion Pod / Van):** Reimbursed at **$0.67 per mile**, tracked via the van's odometer, which Stuart insists is "possessed" and not merely "driven badly by Stuart."
- **Airfare:** Economy under 6 hours; premium economy for longer flights, pre-approved, and NO, "the rocket" does not count as economy, that is a whole different budget line and you know it.
- **Lodging:** Reasonable rates; itemized folio required, including the $30 in-room banana snacks we already know you bought.
- **Meals while traveling:** Per diem **$75/day** domestic, **$95/day** international, tips included. No reimbursement for "emotional support tacos," even during a difficult mission.
- **Corporate card:** Required for missions with anticipated expenses over $1,000. Personal card usage on major missions is a fast track to Villain Protocol.

## 6. Home Pod Equipment Stipend

Remote/hybrid Minions get **$500 annual stipend** for pod furniture, lighting, ergonomic goggle stands, etc. Stipend may NOT be used to purchase second freeze ray "for home testing purposes." We have had this conversation. It did not go well the first time either.

## 7. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Never reimbursable:
- Personal entertainment (this includes the banana piñata, no, we don't care whose birthday it was)
- Parking/traffic tickets (the van gets a LOT of these)
- Family travel costs
- Weaponized gadgets purchased "just in case, seemed useful"
- Unauthorized shrink-ray test subjects, including but not limited to: the office plant, Kevin's hat, and, on one occasion, a neighboring villain's car

## 8. Client / Rival Villain Entertainment

Meetings with rival villains, meant to gather intel, reimbursable up to $150/person, with names and organizations documented, unless meeting devolves into "who has the better lair," in which case that portion is on you personally.

## 9. Correction Protocol

Submitted expense with error — wrong amount, accidentally expensed a stolen diamond as "office supplies"? File **Correction Protocol** within 5 business days. No penalty for honest mistakes. Repeated "accidental" stolen diamonds will be reviewed with significant, deserved suspicion.

## 10. Fraud and Misuse

Falsified, inflated, or duplicate claims mean immediate disciplinary action, escalation to Gru personally, and possible reassignment to Fluffy Unicorn Storage Duty, which sounds nice, is not nice, ask anyone who's done it.

## 11. Questions

Direct questions to Dr. Nefario, who is tired, but in an evil-genius way, which is a different kind of tired than yours.
