# Training Policy (for Minions)

**Organization:** Gru's Evil Lair & Associates
**Document ID:** LAIR-POL-018
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2024
**Last Reviewed:** February 1, 2024
**Owner:** Dr. Nefario (the slides are forty years old and still accurate)

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Minion learns to do dangerous things safely, or at least documentarily. Every skill in the lair was invented by someone who did it wrong first. Training exists so the wrong part happens to a mannequin.

## 2. Onboarding

New Minions complete a structured onboarding program covering lair layout, safety, and the difference between doors marked "push" and doors marked "it pushed back once." The Remote Work Policy (LAIR-POL-014) requires the full in-lair onboarding period before any remote arrangement, and no, onboarding does not count double if Minion does it enthusiastically.

## 3. Safety Certifications

Safety certification is required before working near the freeze-ray range, the vat room, or the parking structure (see the incident log, all of it, one incident, ongoing). Certifications renew annually. Expiration notices are sent twice; the third notice is delivered by Gru standing silently beside Minion's workstation.

## 4. Gadget Authorization

Equipment authorization has three levels. Level 1 covers standard kit. Level 2 covers ray-class equipment per the Equipment Policy (LAIR-POL-016). Level 3 covers the magnet boots, which almost nobody passes, because the final exam is theoretical, practical, and slightly philosophical.

## 5. Continuing Villain Education

Each Minion attends at least two refresher seminars per year from the catalog: "Exits: A Reference Guide," "Passwords Are Not Snacks," "Tarp Fundamentals," and "Debriefs Without Blame (There Will Be Blame)." Attendance is logged and lukewarm applause is noted. Seminar content is updated whenever reality gets ahead of the slides, which reality does roughly once a quarter, usually near the vat.

## 6. Failure and Retakes

Failing a certification is not a penalty; failing it theatrically becomes one. One retake per quarter is allowed. A second failure means a remedial plan, a mentor assignment, and a frank conversation about whether the jetpack is really Minion's calling. The mentor is not a punishment for the mentor. The mentor is an honor. The mentor has been informed of this twice and remains unconvinced.

## 7. Training During Work Hours

Scheduled training occurs during paid work hours. Minion does not train "off the clock to get ahead," because unsupervised practice is how the lair acquired its second loading dock, briefly, at a different location. Practice in the designated simulation bay is always allowed, is still training, and is still on the clock, which is the lair's way of saying it would rather pay for the mannequin than the window.

## 8. Records

The training ledger records certifications, expirations, and seminar attendance. Falsifying a record goes straight to the disciplinary process in the Expense Policy (LAIR-POL-009), because a minion who lies about jetpack class will lie about a receipt.

## 9. Questions

Direct questions to Dr. Nefario before the lecture, during the lecture, or after the lecture, but never during the slide about the vat, which he considers self-explanatory and harrowing.
