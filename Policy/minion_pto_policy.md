# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy (for Minions)

**Organization:** Gru's Evil Lair & Associates
**Document ID:** LAIR-POL-011
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Last Reviewed:** January 1, 2024
**Owner:** Gru (with reluctant help from Dr. Nefario, who actually files the forms)

## 1. Purpose

Light bulb! This policy explains how Minion earns time off, how Minion requests it, and how Minion does NOT simply disappear into the banana fields for "personal reasons" without telling anyone, again, Kevin, we are talking about you.

## 2. PTO Accrual

Full-time Minions accrue **1.67 days per month**, totaling **20 days per year**. Part-time Minions (currently: none, everyone here is very full-time, very committed, slightly too committed) accrue proportionally. Cannot use PTO in first 30 days, so no, you cannot request "Day One Nap" as vacation, Stuart, we discussed this.

## 3. PTO Categories

Covers vacation, personal time, routine goggle/eye checkups, bereavement (Section 7), and personal days for any reason Minion does not wish to disclose, INCLUDING "I need day to just yell for a while," which we are told is a legitimate Minion emotional need and, fine, we accept this.

Illness lasting more than three days falls under Short-Term Disability, which mostly means Dr. Nefario examines you and mutters about "reversing whatever THIS is."

## 4. Requesting Time Off

Request PTO at least **5 business days** in advance. Same-day illness/emergency requests may be filed retroactively — this includes emergencies such as "accidentally shrunk self, need day to normalize," which yes, has happened, yes, there is now a form for it specifically.

Squad Leaders approve or deny within 2 business days. Silence does not equal approval. It also does not mean Squad Leader "forgot," Squad Leaders do not get to use that excuse anymore either.

## 5. Blackout Periods

No PTO requests during active heist windows or declared Villain Protocol lockdowns, announced 30 days in advance where possible (rarely possible, we are villains, plans change). Attempting to sneak a vacation request in during active lockdown triggers immediate escalation to Gru, whose response will simply be a long, disappointed silence, which is somehow worse than yelling.

## 6. Carryover and Payout

Carry over up to **5 unused PTO days** into next year; excess forfeited December 31st except where required by law or by extremely persuasive Minion puppy-eyes, which carries no legal weight but works on Gru anyway, don't tell him we said that either.

## 7. Bereavement Leave

**5 paid days** for immediate family, **2 paid days** for extended family or, per lair precedent, one (1) beloved banana that was "like a brother, honestly." No Joke Protocol jokes during bereavement. This is the one rule nobody has ever broken. We remain, as an organization, proud of this.

## 8. Holidays

11 paid lair holidays per year, including Gru's Birthday (mandatory attendance, mandatory enthusiasm, mandatory pretending the cake is not, once again, banana flavored).

## 9. Correction Protocol

Filed PTO with an error — wrong dates, wrong reason, listed "shrink ray recovery" when it was actually "slept through alarm"? File **Correction Protocol** within 5 business days. No penalty for honest mistakes. We will still ask what really happened. We already suspect it was the alarm.

## 10. Policy Violations

Excessive unplanned absences or falsified reasons may result in disciplinary action, up to and including a very long, very quiet conversation with Gru that somehow feels worse than any punishment previously invented by prior villain regimes.

## 11. Questions

Direct questions to Gru or Dr. Nefario. Gru will answer sternly. He will still mean it kindly. We all know this. Nobody says it out loud.
