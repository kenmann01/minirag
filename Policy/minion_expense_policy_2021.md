# Expense Reimbursement Policy (for Minions)

**Organization:** Gru's Evil Lair & Associates
**Document ID:** LAIR-POL-009
**Effective Date:** June 1, 2021
**Last Reviewed:** June 1, 2021
**Owner:** Dr. Nefario (younger, marginally less tired)

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains what lair will pay Minion back for. 2021 note: this was written before the Second Sub-Basement Incident, before the Unicorn Storage Facility existed, before we knew what Stuart was capable of with a company card. Innocent times.

## 2. General Principles

All expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and have receipt. Expenses under $20 need only written description.

## 3. Submission and Deadlines

Expense reports submitted within **90 days**. We were relaxed about this then. We had not yet learned why we should not be.

## 4. Approval Thresholds

- **Under $250:** Squad Leader approval only.
- **$250–$1,000:** Squad Leader + Dr. Nefario approval.
- **Over $1,000:** Gru approval required.

## 5. Travel Expenses

- **Mileage (Minion Pod / Van):** Reimbursed at **$0.56 per mile**.
- **Airfare:** Economy under 6 hours; premium economy for longer flights, pre-approved.
- **Lodging:** Reasonable rates; itemized folio required.
- **Meals while traveling:** Per diem **$60/day** domestic, **$80/day** international, tips included.

## 6. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Never reimbursable:
- Personal entertainment
- Parking/traffic tickets
- Family travel costs
- Weaponized gadgets purchased "just in case"

## 7. Client / Rival Villain Entertainment

Meetings with rival villains reimbursable up to $100/person, names and organizations documented.

## 8. Fraud and Misuse

Falsified, inflated, or duplicate claims mean immediate disciplinary action and escalation to Gru personally.

## 9. Questions

Direct questions to Dr. Nefario, back when there was only one sub-basement to worry about.
