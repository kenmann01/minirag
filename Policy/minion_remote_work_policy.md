# Remote Work Policy (for Minions)

**Organization:** Gru's Evil Lair & Associates (formerly Evil, now... light evil. Freelance evil.)
**Document ID:** LAIR-POL-014
**Effective Date:** the day after Kevin flooded the sub-basement
**Last Reviewed:** by Gru, personally, with much sighing
**Owner:** Gru (nobody else can read the handwriting)

## 1. Purpose

Light bulb! This policy is for explaining when Minion is allowed to work from home lair instead of main lair, because after the Incident With the Fridge, some things must be written down now, in words, so nobody can say "you never told us."

## 2. Eligibility

Is available to all Minions except ones currently on Freeze Ray Duty, ones currently ON FIRE, and Stuart, who asked to work remotely "so I can wear no pants," which — no. Not why remote work is for. New Minions must survive 90 days of in-lair training before working remote, so we know they will not, for example, launch self out of window "by accident, testing something."

## 3. Work Arrangement Types

- **Fully Remote:** Minion works from own pod / secret banana bunker. We do not ask questions about banana bunker. We have learned.
- **Hybrid:** Minimum two days per week in main lair, so Gru remembers Minion's face and also so Minion does not forget how doors work.
- **Occasional Remote:** Minion mostly in lair but sometimes disappears "on mission," which is 60% real mission, 40% found a good taco truck.

## 4. Core Hours

All remote Minions must be reachable 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM lair time. Outside these hours, whereabouts of Minion is between Minion and universe. Gru does not want to know. Gru learned this lesson. Gru learned it very loudly, at 3 AM, involving a kazoo.

## 5. Equipment and Home Lair Setup

Lair provides: one (1) laptop, protected by minimum three layers of banana-proofing. Home workspace must be safe, must be dry, must NOT be inside the industrial freeze-ray testing chamber, no matter how "cozy" Minion finds the ambient temperature. See Expense Policy (LAIR-POL-009) for home-pod stipend.

## 6. Information Security Requirements

Remote Minions must:
- Use lair VPN only. Not the "free WiFi" from van marked SUSPICIOUS VAN, DO NOT ENTER, which someone (Stuart) connected to anyway.
- Enable encryption on device, meaning: do not write lair passwords on banana peel and then eat evidence, this happened, we know it happened, Kevin.
- Never show blueprint of shrink ray to persons outside organization, including but not limited to: pigeons, other villains, Vector, or anyone claiming to be "totally not a spy, why you ask."
- Report lost equipment within one hour. Reporting "equipment was eaten" requires additional form.

Violation of this section triggers **Villain Protocol** (our version of Nuke Protocol, except with more actual danger): full lockdown, Gru personally reviewing security footage, and a mandatory re-training seminar titled "Passwords Are Not Snacks."

## 7. Communication Expectations

Keep calendar updated. Respond to messages within one business day, unless business day involves being frozen, melted, or launched. Camera must be on for lair-wide meetings, UNLESS Minion currently disguised as human for undercover mission, in which case camera stays off and nobody is to comment on the very convincing mustache.

## 8. International Remote Work

Working remote from another country (or another dimension, we've had one incident) requires 30 days advance notice to Gru and Dr. Nefario, for tax reasons, passport reasons, and "did you bring back the correct number of Minions" reasons.

## 9. Performance and Reviews

Remote arrangement continues as long as mission success rate stays above acceptable villain-adjacent standard. Poor performance may result in return to in-lair work with two weeks notice, except immediate Villain Protocol violations, which skip notice entirely and go straight to Gru's Disappointed Face, which is worse than any punishment we could write down.

## 10. Policy Review

Reviewed whenever something explodes that shouldn't have. Questions may be directed to Gru, who will answer in a voice that sounds stern but is, underneath, very fond of all of you, don't tell him we said that.
