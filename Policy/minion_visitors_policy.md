# Visitors Policy (for Minions)

**Organization:** Gru's Evil Lair & Associates
**Document ID:** LAIR-POL-020
**Effective Date:** March 15, 2024
**Last Reviewed:** March 15, 2024
**Owner:** Dr. Nefario (the sign-in sheet is older than most of the lair)

## 1. Purpose

This policy covers humans and others who enter the lair on purpose, with permission, and on the books. The lair has a front door now. It has opinions about how it is used.

## 2. Approved Visitor Types

Approved visitors: contracted scientists, delivery personnel, repair technicians, inspectors (sigh), and Minion family members on designated tour days. Unapproved: everyone else, including "a guy Kevin knows," "a very trustworthy pigeon," and anyone who arrives describing themselves as "basically family."

## 3. Sponsorship and Approval

Every visit has a Minion sponsor who files the request with Dr. Nefario at least two business days in advance, listing the visitor's name, purpose, and expected duration. Sponsorship means responsibility: the sponsor meets the visitor at the door, walks the visitor out, and writes the incident report if there is one, in the first person, without the word "suddenly."

## 4. Escort Requirements

Visitors are escorted at all times at a ratio of one Minion per visitor. Escorted means accompanied, not "watched from across the room while gesturing occasionally." Visitors do not hold doors, press buttons, or "just peek" into anything with a light above it.

## 5. Non-Disclosure

Every visitor signs the non-disclosure agreement before passing the second airlock. The NDA is short, binding, and available in nine languages; the banana-stain on the Dalmatian translation is a printing artifact and is not, as rumored, Dr. Nefario's signature.

## 6. Tour Routes and Restricted Zones

Public tours follow the marked route: lobby, Hall of Previous Plans, gift shop. Restricted zones are the vat room, the sub-basements, the catwalks, and Gru's parking structure, which contains a car, a grudge, and no visitors, ever.

## 7. Delivery Personnel

Deliveries use the loading dock entrance. Drivers remain with the vehicle; Minion unloads. Any driver who "helps carry it in" is thanked warmly, walked out gently, and added to the watch list with a small sticker indicating enthusiasm. Packages with ticking, humming, or "faint villainous chuckling" sounds go to Dr. Nefario unopened, from a distance, using the cart.

## 8. Minion Family Visits

Family tour days run the last Friday of each month. Families see the approved route, meet the Squad Leader, and take one group photo in front of the mural. Grandmothers may pinch cheeks. Grandmothers may not pinch Gru. One tried. We do not repeat the details here or anywhere.

## 9. Incidents Involving Visitors

If a visitor sees something restricted, the sponsor reports it to Dr. Nefario within one hour, the visitor gets a follow-up conversation, and the tour route gets shorter. This has happened four times. The route used to include a gift shop, a fountain, and two bridges.

## 10. Questions

Direct questions to Dr. Nefario or the front desk, which cannot answer questions but will accept compliments about the door.
