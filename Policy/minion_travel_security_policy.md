# Travel Security Policy (for Minions)

**Organization:** Gru's Evil Lair & Associates
**Document ID:** LAIR-POL-015
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024
**Last Reviewed:** March 1, 2024
**Owner:** Dr. Nefario (Gru reviews it from a helicopter, waving)

## 1. Purpose

This policy keeps Minion alive, un-kidnapped, and un-photographed while traveling on lair business. The lair has lost more minions to bad vacation selfies than to rival villains, and the rival villains find this insulting.

## 2. Mission Clearance

Travel on lair business requires a Mission Clearance stamped by the Squad Leader before departure, not after, and not "on the way." Clearance levels are Green (routine errands), Yellow (heist support), and Banana (anything involving Vector, who is not clever but is persistent, like a raccoon with a law degree).

## 3. Itineraries and Cover Stories

Every trip files a written itinerary with the Squad Leader. Cover stories must be plausible: "sightseeing," "conference," and "definitely not picking anything up" are pre-approved. "I improvised" is not an itinerary, Dave, and the Perth incident is exactly why it is not.

## 4. Transport Standards

Ground transport uses the Minion Van, odometer logged before and after; mileage reimbursement lives in the Expense Policy (LAIR-POL-009). Jetpacks require a current certification from the Training Policy (LAIR-POL-018). The rocket remains reserved for Gru. The answer has not changed since Tuesday, and it will not change on Thursday either.

## 5. Lodging and Safe Houses

Overnight stays use approved safe houses only. Motels advertising hourly rates are automatically disqualified, as are any lodgings described by the owner as "quirky," "off the books," or "you didn't hear it from me." Bolt the door, test the window, and never tell the front desk "no one will look for me here."

## 6. Lockdown Drills and the Villain Protocol

The lair runs one unannounced lockdown drill per quarter. When the klaxon sounds, Minion walks, does not run, to Sub-Basement B (the dry one), sits at the assigned bunk with Minion's name taped on it, and waits for the Squad Leader headcount. Bunk reassignment requests go to Dr. Nefario and are denied. Hard hats stay on until the headcount finishes. Talking during the headcount counts as not being present, which counts as missing the drill, which is a seminar. Attendance at the post-drill seminar, "Exits: A Reference Guide," is mandatory, logged, and catered, and the caterer is not to be blamed for the klaxon schedule. The same bunks, headcount, and seminar apply whenever the Villain Protocol is triggered for real, which happens for unauthorized weapon prototype use, a confirmed breach of the Remote Work Policy (LAIR-POL-014) security rules, an intruder past the perimeter, theft or loss of any blueprint, or any Minion presumed abducted. Once it is triggered, Dr. Nefario is not exempt, no matter what he says, and Gru reads the headcount himself.

## 7. Abduction and Ransom Etiquette

If abducted: cooperate, memorize faces, and do not negotiate on the lair's behalf. The lair does not pay ransoms above operating budget; it sends a strongly worded letter and, if that fails, Gru. Historically, Gru arrives before the letter does.

## 8. Lost or Stolen Equipment Abroad

Report lost or stolen gadgets to the Squad Leader by end of shift with a written account of the previous four hours. Remote Minions follow the one-hour rule in the Remote Work Policy (LAIR-POL-014) instead. A report stating "a bird took it" must include the bird's approximate wingspan.

## 9. Post-Mission Debriefs

Every mission ends with a debrief within two business days, covering what happened, what nearly happened, and whose fault the banana part was. Honest debriefs carry no penalty. A debrief where nobody admits the banana part carries a penalty for everyone present.

## 10. Questions

Direct questions to Dr. Nefario, who has pre-printed forms for most disasters and is quietly drafting new ones every time Stuart leaves the building.
