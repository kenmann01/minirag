# Equipment Policy (for Minions)

**Organization:** Gru's Evil Lair & Associates
**Document ID:** LAIR-POL-016
**Effective Date:** February 15, 2024
**Last Reviewed:** February 15, 2024
**Owner:** Dr. Nefario (custody of the clipboard is custody of the arsenal)

## 1. Purpose

This policy covers how Minion borrows, returns, maintains, and occasionally explains lair equipment. The lair owns many valuable machines. The lair also employs many Minions. History shows these two facts require paperwork.

## 2. Standard Issue Kit

Every Minion receives one (1) pair of goggles, two (2) sets of overalls, and one (1) banana-shaped multitool. Replacement goggles are issued free twice per year; after that, Minion writes an essay titled "Where The Last Pair Went" and reads it aloud at the squad meeting.

## 3. Checkout and Return

Equipment beyond the standard kit is checked out through the armory ledger: tag number, purpose, and expected return, written in pen, in Minion's own handwriting, legibly enough that the armory team is not forced to guess. Checkout periods run 72 hours, extendable once in writing. Returning equipment "mostly working" is not returning equipment, it is donating a problem to the next Minion, and the ledger remembers who donated it.

## 4. Restricted Equipment

Freeze rays, shrink rays, magnet boots, and anything else with the word "ray" in its inventory tag require Dr. Nefario's signature and a Level 2 certification from the Training Policy (LAIR-POL-018). Possession without either triggers review under the Travel Security Policy (LAIR-POL-015) and a conversation nobody enjoys.

## 5. Maintenance and Inspections

Lair equipment is inspected quarterly by the armory team. Minion performs basic cleaning only: no disassembly, no "improvements," and absolutely no swapping parts between two broken items to build what Kevin called "a better one." It was not better. The medbay has the report.

## 6. Damage and Loss Reporting

Damage is reported to the Squad Leader by end of shift with the equipment tag number and an honest account. Honest damage reports carry no penalty; the lair expects some scorch marks, this is a villain organization, not a library. Loss reports follow the timeline in the policy governing Minion's location at the time.

## 7. Custom Modifications

Custom modifications to lair equipment are banned, including "just a small one," "purely cosmetic lasers," and anything involving plumbing the jetpack into the espresso machine. Dave's original diagram is framed in the break room as a warning, next to the scorch mark it explains.

## 8. Decommissioning

Broken equipment goes to the decommission bin, never the trash, never the "free to a good home" table Stuart invented without permission. Decommissioned items are stripped for parts by the armory team, who know exactly which parts fit where and are tired of finding them in the vending machine.

## 9. Personal Equipment

Personal gadgets may not be used on lair business. The lair cannot insure what it does not own, and it cannot explain to a rival villain why their doomsday device was defeated by a modified garage-door opener that Minion "had lying around." It happened. It will not happen again.

## 10. Questions

Direct questions to the armory team, or to Dr. Nefario if the armory team is visibly hiding.
